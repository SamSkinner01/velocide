# Premise: Shrikes, Moulds and the Kiln

The world, who the players are, and what the core mechanics mean in it. This replaces the first
premise ("Shrikes are chrome-plated, blood-fuelled machines" that heal by standing in enemies'
blood), which sat too close to another game's. Mechanics and numbers do not change; their
fiction, names, visuals and sounds do.

## The pitch

*The Foundry never stopped pouring. Every scrap of heat left in the world has been cast into
its Moulds — and the Shrikes, whose lives are a flame behind a mask, are fast enough to take it
back.*

## Shrikes (the players)

- Lean, fast hunters in **beaked porcelain masks** (the shrike is the butcher bird) over layered
  leather and brass harness, long coat tails, wrapped forearms and gloves. Not robots, no
  chrome.
- Each carries its life as a **wick**: a flame burning in a caged lantern set into its chest,
  showing through the mask's eye slits. Nobody knows what is under the mask; the flame is all
  that shows.
- Damage **gutters** the wick; dying snuffs it. Team colour tints the wick flame and the mask's
  trim, so players read at a glance.

## Moulds (the enemies)

- Things poured and set in the Foundry: grey **wax-flesh**, slag, brass and chain, animated by
  the heat the Foundry pours into them. They glow from inside where that heat collects (weak
  points, telegraphs).
- They **don't bleed**. A hit cracks them and sprays **molten wax and sparks**; a kill slumps,
  splits and melts them, releasing their heat in one big burst. Decals are wax splats and scorch
  marks, not blood.

## Mechanics in the fiction

| mechanic (unchanged numbers) | old fiction | new fiction |
|---|---|---|
| close-range healing (`docs/gameplay.md`) | blood bursts heal whoever stands closest | **ember bursts**: every hit sheds embers at the hit point; the closest Shrike within reach **draws them in** (a stream of sparks into its chest lantern) — *kindling* |
| big burst on a kill | death blood burst | **heat release**: the Mould's stored heat, a big ember burst |
| hard damage | (unnamed) | **smother**: part of each hit smothers the wick and caps max health until it rekindles |
| parry reward (full heal) | — | the wick **flares**: white flash, full rekindle |
| PvP: players hit each other | blood | sparks and ash from the other Shrike's wick; their embers can be stolen |
| style rank 7 | SANGUINE (crimson) | **SEARING** (crimson-orange) |

## Places

The Foundry: kilns, crucibles, wax vats, slag channels, bell towers and cisterns. Molten pools
are **molten slag/metal**, not blood; level names and room names avoid blood words
("Crucible of Veins" stays — ore veins).

## Words to avoid

blood, bleed, gore, veins-as-blood, sanguine, flesh-as-meat, "blood-fuelled", "machine",
"chrome" (for Shrikes). Use: heat, embers, wick, kindle, smother, wax, slag, scorch, sparks.
