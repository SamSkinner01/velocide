VELOCIDE  (build @VERSION@)
==========================

START THE GAME
  Windows: double-click velocide.exe
    If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
    This happens because the game isn't code-signed.
    A black console window opens next to the game. It shows the game's log; you can ignore it,
    but if something goes wrong, send a screenshot of it.
  Linux:   run ./velocide from this folder
    You need a working Vulkan driver (on Debian/Ubuntu: mesa-vulkan-drivers, or the NVIDIA
    driver).

  Keep the "assets" folder next to the game. The game won't find its levels without it.

PLAY WITH FRIENDS
  Join:  Main menu -> JOIN GAME -> type the address you were given, e.g. 203.0.113.7:47420
  Host:  Main menu -> HOST GAME. Your friends need your IP address, and port 47420 must be
         forwarded on your router (UDP). Using a dedicated server avoids all of that.
  While everyone gathers you can run around the warm-up hall. Tab opens the lobby panel,
  F1 readies you up. LOADOUT picks your weapons, abilities and knack, CUSTOMISE your look.
  Everyone must use the same build (the version above). After an update, everyone downloads
  the new one.

CONTROLS (rebind them in Settings -> Controls)
  WASD move        Mouse look       Space jump / wall jump     Shift dash
  Ctrl or C        slide on the ground, slam in the air
  Left mouse       fire             Right mouse  alt fire
  1 2 3 4          your loadout's weapons (press the same number again to switch ability)
  F punch / parry  Q knack (pick it in LOADOUT)    E use / revive a teammate
  Tab scoreboard (lobby panel in the warm-up)       F1 ready up in the warm-up
  T chat           Esc pause        F11 fullscreen
  Gamepads work too.

TIPS
  Never stop moving: slides keep their speed, and slide-jumps and dash-jumps carry momentum.
  Slam (crouch in the air), then jump right after landing for a huge bounce.
  Every hit sheds embers. Stay close and your wick draws them in: that is how you heal.
  Punch an incoming projectile just before it hits you: it flies back at double damage and
  your wick flares back to full.
  Knack Thorn (solo / co-op): throw it, then shoot it with the Quill: the shot ricochets to
  the nearest enemy. Other knacks heal, trap, blind Moulds, toughen you or speed you up.
  Down in co-op? Watch a teammate: Fire / Alt fire switch, Jump for the chase camera.
  Grind: endless waves until your whole team is down. Gun game: every kill with your current
  weapon moves you up the ladder; the final tier is the Trimmer, and a kill with it wins.

Settings are saved in %APPDATA%\velocide (Windows) or ~/.config/velocide (Linux).
